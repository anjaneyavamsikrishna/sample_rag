NIMBUS TECH - COMPANY HANDBOOK

About us
Nimbus Tech Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2015 and builds cloud analytics software for retail companies.
Our headquarters is in Hyderabad. We also have offices in Bengaluru and Pune. We have around 420 employees.

Working hours
The standard working day is 9:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a 45-minute lunch break.
Everyone must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00.

Hybrid working
Nimbus Tech follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office on three days a week: Tuesday, Wednesday
and Thursday. Mondays and Fridays are flexible work-from-home days with manager agreement.

Holidays and vacation
Nimbus Tech observes 12 public holidays a year; the list is published each December on the intranet.
The office is also closed for the year-end shutdown from 26 December to 31 December. Shutdown days do not
count against your vacation balance. For how much vacation you can take, and what happens to unused days,
please read the Leave Policy.

Probation and notice period
New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. The notice period after confirmation is 60 days.

Dress code
Business casual applies Monday to Thursday. Casual dress is allowed on Fridays. Client-facing staff should
wear business attire on days with client meetings.

Office facilities
The cafeteria on the ground floor of the Hyderabad office serves lunch from 12:30 to 14:30. Free parking is
available for employees who commute by car; register your vehicle with Facilities. The Bengaluru and Pune
offices have pantries but no cafeterias.

Who to contact
HR helpdesk: hr@nimbustech.example, extension 4100.
IT helpdesk: it-help@nimbustech.example, extension 4200. For a lost laptop or phone, call the IT helpdesk
for a replacement device after reporting the loss as described in the IT Security Policy.
Facilities and security desk: extension 4300.

Performance reviews
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in April and October. Salary revisions are decided after the
October review and take effect from 1 January.
